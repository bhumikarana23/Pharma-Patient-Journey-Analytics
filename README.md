# Pharma Patient Journey Analytics: Treatment Switching & Patient Segmentation

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **patient treatment journeys** to understand therapy switching and discontinuation patterns in a pharmaceutical/healthcare context. The project applies data analytics and data science techniques to identify factors associated with treatment changes, assess switching risk, and segment patients based on treatment behavior.

The objective is to demonstrate an end-to-end **healthcare data science workflow** combining data preparation, statistical analysis, machine learning, patient segmentation, and visualization.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze patient treatment pathways and switching patterns.
* Identify key factors associated with therapy switching or discontinuation.
* Explore relationships between patient characteristics and treatment outcomes.
* Develop a model to estimate treatment-switching risk.
* Segment patients based on treatment behavior and characteristics.
* Present analytical findings through interactive visualizations.

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Patient Journey Analysis

Analyze treatment pathways to understand:

* Common treatment sequences
* Treatment duration
* Therapy switching patterns
* Treatment discontinuation
* Patient characteristics associated with switching

### 2. Exploratory & Statistical Analysis

Investigate relationships between treatment switching and variables such as:

* Age
* Disease characteristics
* Treatment type
* Treatment duration
* Previous therapies
* Clinical characteristics

Statistical techniques are used to determine whether observed relationships are meaningful.

### 3. Treatment Switching Prediction

Develop machine learning models to estimate the probability of a patient switching therapy.

Potential models include:

* Logistic Regression
* Random Forest

Model performance will be evaluated using appropriate classification metrics.

### 4. Patient Segmentation

Use clustering techniques to identify groups of patients with similar treatment behavior and characteristics.

This can help identify patient segments with different switching patterns and risk profiles.

### 5. Visualization & Insights

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** will be developed to communicate:

* Patient journey patterns
* Treatment switching rates
* Patient segments
* Key switching factors
* Model-related insights
